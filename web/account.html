Your data

Your email is used to verify login. Your profile stores your name, username, and optional birthdate. Your tasks and profile are stored in this app’s Supabase project and restricted to your account.

This device also stores your session and an account-specific offline copy. Signing out hides your planner but retains drafts for your next login. Avoid signing in on shared devices. You can export your planner or delete your account in Account settings.

Synced changes retain the last ten revisions for recovery. Export backups regularly; no storage service can guarantee that data will never be lost.
